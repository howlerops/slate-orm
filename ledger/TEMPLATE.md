## What changed

One paragraph. The diff has the detail; this is the shape of it.

## Why

What was wrong, or what became possible. If this is a bug fix, describe the
failure — the wrong answer, not the stack trace. If nothing was wrong, say what
this buys and who for.

## Alternatives rejected

What else would have worked, and what it would have cost. This is the section
that is worth the most in a year and the one most likely to be skipped. If
there was genuinely one option, write that and say why the others are not
options.

## Evidence

The measurement, the failing test, the mutation that got caught. Numbers with
their spread, not a single figure. `n/a` for changes that cannot have evidence,
which is rarer than it looks.

## What this does not do

Where the fix stops. Known gaps, cases deliberately left alone, anything that
looks covered and is not.
