    This is slate's own kernel compiled to WebAssembly — the planner, the executor and an in-memory store, running in this tab. There is no server.

    The data is real: 100,000 New York yellow-taxi trips from January 2024, sampled from the TLC's published month, joined to their own 265-zone lookup. Fares, tips and missing passenger counts are as published.

    slate has no SQL. What you type is parsed into its query spec, the same structure the Python, Go and TypeScript clients send, and the Spec tab shows what your statement compiled to.

    Writes land in this tab only. Reload, or press Reset data, to get the fixture back.
